# Module 02: Clock & Trigger Lab

Clock & Trigger Lab teaches how repeating time produces related timing signals.

## Learning outcomes

- convert BPM to frequency and beat duration
- distinguish a gate from a trigger
- hear and see pulse width
- divide and multiply a master clock
- understand swing as alternating long and short intervals
- reset related outputs to a shared starting point
- use manual triggers without changing the master clock

## Outputs

- base clock
- variable-width gate
- short trigger
- divided clock: ÷1, ÷2, ÷4 or ÷8
- multiplied clock: ×1, ×2, ×4 or ×8

## Audio safety

Audio starts only after explicit user interaction. Clicks use short envelopes and a shared low master gain. Panic / stop disconnects the master output and closes the AudioContext.

## Completion boundary

The module deliberately does not include sequencing, probability, Euclidean rhythms, external MIDI clock or network sync. Those belong in later focused modules.
